VERSION 1 — data-first (recommended)
===================================

STREAM just got a supply cap. Permanently.

The Streamflow Foundation burned 100% of its STREAM — 699.99M tokens, 70% of the total supply.

Supply before: 1,000,000,000
Supply after: 300,000,000

The part that matters: there is no mint authority. That number can't move again.

Proof on-chain:
https://solscan.io/tx/4rbViHbmCV35ttMngeC8KBULCMctD7X8mNg3RxfvGwPKyPi7hX8VKpjyJ81AnYuVzzXEsWuNuppcpDY47hnchZNv?cluster=

What remains, as a share of the original supply:
• 11.47% vesting contracts
• 11.08% free circulation
• 4.03% reserves
• 3.42% active staking rewards

What most burns don't have: a founder who gave back ~90% of his personal allocation — part burned, part returned to the team bucket. All verifiable on-chain.

And this is a token decision, not a product one. Vesting, staking, locks, airdrops, payouts — all unchanged. Use the app.
