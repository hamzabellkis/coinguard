About

Cookie Chain is a Solana Virtual Machine chain: sub-second finality, very low fees, SPL and Token-2022 support, and community-run infrastructure. CoinGuard is an open-source console for it — connect a Nightly wallet, watch the chain, and send a transaction without leaving the page.
